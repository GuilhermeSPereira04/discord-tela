Source commit: 3d3278a0f925aebddc574dfb7042eb1eb815f427

Adaptations: compact worker renderer, smaller terrain bake/render targets and
shadow map, responsive 96x48 / 128x64 character grids, visibility/capture lifecycle,
build-time terrain/color baking and compact vegetation-placement data,
512px detail texture variants. Original scene,
vegetation, water, camera, fox, particles and material-specific ASCII retained.

Textures: Poly Haven (CC0 1.0).
leafy_grass, coast_sand_01, aerial_rocks_02, castle_wall_slates,
weathered_planks, bark_brown_02.
https://polyhaven.com/license

Fox rigging and animation: tomkranis (2014), CC BY 4.0.
glTF conversion: @AsoboStudio and @scurest (2017), CC BY 4.0.
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
